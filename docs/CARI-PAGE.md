# Cari case study

Public route: `/projects/cari/`. Source: `projects/cari/index.html`.

Built from Figma desktop `76:1840` and mobile `564:45`. Includes overview, problem, research, solution, catching up, checking decisions, projects and people, design system, reflection, and the shared contact section. The standalone Prototype section in Figma contains an unfinished placeholder; the working prototype link is included in Catching up instead.

Page styles: `css/cari.css`, with shared `css/styles.css` and `css/conversion.css`. Behavior: `js/cari.js`, with shared `js/main.js`. Text is native HTML; composed visual figures preserve the Figma artwork layout and scale within their containers.

Seven supplied MP4s are connected with JPEG poster fallbacks. Website copies retain the original dimensions and use H.264, yuv420p, and fast-start metadata. The player enables the Figma playback controls, loops visible clips, pauses offscreen and in hidden tabs, and honors reduced motion. Source videos remain excluded from Git. Compression details are recorded in `assets/videos/cari/manifest.json`.

The page was checked at 320, 390, 760, 1024 and 1440 px for horizontal overflow and broken images. The source Figma links and research-source URLs are preserved.
