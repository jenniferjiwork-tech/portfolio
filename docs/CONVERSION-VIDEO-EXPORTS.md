## Export settings

- MP4, 30 FPS, High quality. Export the sharp version, as you did for the homepage; original file size is not the website size target.
- Include the animation panel's full coloured background and crop. Leave the written headings, captions, and surrounding page outside the export.
- Keep the full timeline for each export. Browse, Shop the Look and Quick add share timing in Figma; I will trim any leading/trailing holds and connect them in sequence after inspecting the files.
- For collection pages, export both screens together as one panel. For More styles & sizes, export Before and After separately.
- Hero and desktop Shop the Look are separate exports because their frames have different shapes.
- Export at a resolution that stays sharp at its displayed size. If Figma limits your export dimensions, use the highest allowed size without changing the frame's aspect ratio.

Once the files are added, tell me. I will preserve all originals, compress separate website copies, compare visual quality, connect playback, and check the sequence and image fallbacks. Compression will prioritize sharpness rather than forcing every clip below an arbitrary size.
